# RuralClass Translation Review & Quality Tracker

This document records localized phrases flagged for review by native classroom teachers and curriculum specialists. All translations use natural pedagogical phrasing and keep Western Arabic numerals (0–9) across all three languages.

## Review Guidelines
1. Tone must be warm, supportive, and practical for a government/rural primary school teacher.
2. Avoid literal word-for-word machine translation.
3. Keep mathematical symbols and numerals in standard Western format: `0-9`, `1/2`, `₹10`, `%`.
